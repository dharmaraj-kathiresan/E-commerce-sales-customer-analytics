E-Commerce Sales & Customer Analytics

Project Overview

This project analyzes e-commerce sales, customer purchasing behavior, payment methods, product performance, and delivery performance using SQL and Power BI.

Tools Used

- PostgreSQL
- SQL
- Power BI
- Microsoft Excel (if used for this project)

Business Questions

1. What is the total sales revenue and average order value?
2. How do sales change month by month?
3. Which product categories generate the most revenue?
4. Which states contribute the most sales?
5. Which products and sellers perform best?
6. What payment methods do customers use most?
7. How many customers place repeat orders?
8. What percentage of orders are canceled?
9. How long do deliveries take on average?
10. Which states have the highest late-delivery rates?
11. How do delivery times relate to customer review scores?

Key Findings

- Total sales in the current Power BI dashboard: approximately 15.42 million.
- Average order value: approximately 159.83.
- Repeat customer rate: approximately 3.00%.
- Average delivery time: approximately 12.5 days.
- Late delivery rate: approximately 8.11%.
- Average review score for delivered orders: approximately 4.16 out of 5.

These figures reflect the current analysis and may vary with report filters.

Power BI Dashboard

Page 1: Sales Overview

Includes total sales, orders, customers, average order value, monthly sales trends, top product categories, payment methods, order status, and top states by sales.

Page 2: Customer & Delivery Analytics

Includes repeat customer rate, delivery performance, review scores, top customers, top products, and top sellers.
